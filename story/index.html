Creative Work
Based on your personal experiences contributing to society, create a poem, story, song, collage, drawing, painting, photograph, or another creative work that captures the essence of your contributions to others.
In your work, reflect on the people, places, moments, and motivation that have shaped your experiences. Consider how, where, what, and why you chose to contribute and express these elements in a meaningful and creative way.
Some questions to guide your expression:
  • What have you found to be the importance of contributing to others outside your family?
  • What aspects of your identity influence or inform how you contribute?
  • What life experiences have influenced your willingness to give back to others, a community, and/or society?
